# Sidebar cards

On desktop and tablet, the sidebar stacks Saved messages, Chats and your
sections in that order. Chats contains conversations you have not filed in a
section. Pinned conversations in Chats appear once, in the avatar strip above
its rows. Sections follow your chosen order and sort mode. A collapsed section
shows an unread total that excludes muted conversations.

In the collapsed sidebar, each card becomes a group of avatars with unread
badges and accessible names. Expand sidebar restores the full view. On phones,
the conversation list and tab bar remain available, with Saved messages and
pinned chats at the top of the list.

Drag a conversation onto a section to file it, or onto Chats to unfile it. Drop
it on “Drop here for a new section” to open the section editor with that
conversation selected. The row menu’s “Move to section” action provides the same
filing controls with the keyboard.
