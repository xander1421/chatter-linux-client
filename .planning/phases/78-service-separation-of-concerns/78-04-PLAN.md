<objective>
Audit and remove remaining direct repository access from UI layer, routing all storage operations through appropriate services.

Purpose: The UI layer (src/bin_ui/) should never directly access repositories. All storage operations should go through the service layer to ensure business rules are applied consistently and to maintain proper separation of concerns.

Output: Clean UI layer with zero direct repository calls (except where repositories are passed to services).
</objective>

1. Any remaining `message_repo.insert()` calls in bin_ui/
2. Any remaining `channel_repo.update()` or `channel_repo.insert()` calls
3. Any remaining `user_repo` direct mutations
