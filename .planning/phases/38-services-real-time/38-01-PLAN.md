**Established patterns:**
- Services take Arc<Aggregator> for local state, &mut Client for network ops
- Services provide subscribe() returning broadcast::Receiver
- Services delegate local queries to aggregators (no network, no auth check)
- Network operations check auth via settings_repo CURRENT_USER_ID
- TypingAggregator.process(TypingIndicator) routes incoming indicators
- ReadReceiptAggregator.process(ReadReceipt) routes incoming receipts
