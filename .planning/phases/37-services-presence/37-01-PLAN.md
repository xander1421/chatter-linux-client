- LRU cache for presence (PresenceRouter)

**Constraining decisions:**
- Phase 20-03: PresenceRouter uses broadcast capacity 128 with 1000-entry LRU cache

- Domain PresenceStatus enum: Offline, Online, Away, DoNotDisturb
- Proto UserPresence enum: PresenceUnspecified=0, Offline=1, Online=2, Away=3, DoNotDisturb=4
