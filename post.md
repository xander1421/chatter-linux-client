## Privacy by Default

We didn't consent to a world where talking to a friend requires handing over a phone number, a face scan, or an email address.

Privacy is not a feature. It's not a premium tier. It's the natural state of human communication that technology has stolen and sold back to us.

A cryptographic key should be enough. Prove you hold the key. Nothing more.

We build for those who believe identity is what you prove, not what you surrender.
